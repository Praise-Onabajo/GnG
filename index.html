Play Fair & Safe

House Rules

To keep Games n Gadgets safe, fun, and welcoming for everyone, all visitors must follow these four rules.

RULE 01

Zero Tolerance for Violence

Zero tolerance for violence or fighting. Friendly rivalry stays on the screen—any physical aggression results in immediate removal.

RULE 02

Pay Before You Play

Payment must be made before using any equipment. Settle your session via cash or bank transfer at the counter first.

RULE 03

Ages 15 & Above Only

Ages 15 and above only. Our arcade environment and equipment are strictly reserved for guests aged 15+.

RULE 04

Respect Everyone & Gear

Respect the staff, other players, and the equipment. Handle controllers, VR headsets, cues, and paddles with care.
